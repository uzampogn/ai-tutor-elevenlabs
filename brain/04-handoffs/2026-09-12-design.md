### Gotchas learned (this session)

- Two sessions were briefly building in the same working tree; always check `ListAgents` / recent mtimes before continuing another session's work.
- Playwright's `getByText('Copy')` is a substring match — it matched "copying" inside an answer and produced mid-stream captures; wait on `.msg-actions` instead.
- Route handlers registered via `run_code` persist on the page across calls; `page.unrouteAll()` first.
- `useLayoutEffect` in a child runs before the parent's ref attaches on first mount; OrbStage queues a microtask measure for that case.
